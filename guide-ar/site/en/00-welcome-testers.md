# Welcome to the WhyNot Cut 3.0 test {#ch00-title}

Thank you for trying WhyNot Cut before it is released to everyone. This page is just for you as a tester: what to try, what you need, the shortest way to your first reel, and how to tell us what you found. The rest of the guide explains every step in detail.

> **Tip:** **Which sections do you follow?** Most of this guide works for both versions. Where version 3.0 differs from the version you have today (version 2.x), you will see it in the section title or its first sentence, in a tip at the start of the chapter, or in the grey line “In 3.0 — not verified yet”. To find out your version, ask Claude “إيه رقم نسخة WhyNot Cut؟” (“What version of WhyNot Cut do I have?”). If the number starts with 3, follow the 3.0 sections where they differ from today, and the parts with the grey line are exactly what we are asking you to try. If it starts with 2, skip the 3.0 sections until the test version reaches you.

## What we are asking you to try {#ch00-what}

Use WhyNot Cut as you would in your daily work, and try these in order:

1. **Installing it on your computer**, on a Mac first if you have one ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-title)).
2. **Your first reel on the sample client** that comes with WhyNot Cut (next section).
3. **A real client of yours**: setting up the client ([Chapter 5](05-new-client.md#ch05-title)), then editing one video ([Chapter 6](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-title)).
4. **The WhyNot Cut panel in Premiere** ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-title)), and the three tools WhyNot Tidy, WhyNot Hush and WhyNot Kalam (Chapters [11](11-tidy.md#ch11-title), [12](12-hush.md#ch12-title) and [13](13-kalam.md#ch13-title)).
5. **Podcast mode** on a full episode, if you have a podcast ([Chapter 8](08-podcast.md#ch08-title)).

Any sentence in this guide you did not understand, or any step you did not find as written, is as important a note for us as any bug in the software.

## What you need for the test {#ch00-need}

- **Your own Claude account**, which you sign in to yourself in the Claude app. The Pro plan is enough; we recommend the Max plan if you will use it every day.
- **A Mac or Windows computer** that meets the requirements in [the table in Chapter 1](01-intro.md#ch01-requirements), with Premiere Pro under your own licence, plus After Effects if you try motion mode.
- **Magnific**, only if you want to try its image assets ([Chapter 15](15-magnific.md#ch15-title)). Everything else works without it.

## Your first reel in under 10 minutes {#ch00-first-reel}

WhyNot Cut comes with a ready sample client: brand files and a short raw video, so you do not need to prepare anything. The whole path:

1. **Install WhyNot Cut** with the one line ([Install with one line](02-install.md#ch02-one-line)). On a Mac, read [“macOS permissions before they appear”](02-install.md#ch02-mac-permissions) first.
2. **Open a new chat in the Claude app, and choose the sample client folder in it** before the first message ([First time in the Claude app](02-install.md#ch02-claude-first)).
3. **Type “عدّل الفيديوهات الجديدة” (“Edit the new videos”)**, and follow Claude's messages ([Ask for the edit](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-request)).
4. **Watch the preview video and approve the plan** ([Approving the plan in the chat](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-approval-chat)).
5. **Open Premiere Pro and leave it open**, so the project is built inside it ([Building the Premiere project](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-build)).
6. **Read the status report**: what was verified, what was not verified, and what needs you ([QC and the status report](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-qc)).

> **In 3.0 — not verified yet:** The name and location of the sample client folder, and how long the path takes on your computer, will be written here when the sample client arrives with the test version.

## How to send us what you found {#ch00-report}

- **Before you send**, try [“The first step for any problem”](17-troubleshooting.md#ch17-first); many problems have a short fix.
- **For each problem or note**: ask Claude to sum up what happened and what it tried, then send the summary, with a short description of what you were doing and what happened, to info@thewhynot.ai ([“When the problem isn't solved”](17-troubleshooting.md#ch17-support)).
- **Notes on the guide**: write the page number and the sentence you did not understand, and what you expected to find.

> **In 3.0 — not verified yet:** With the test version you will also get a way to save the diagnostics file and attach it to your notes ([“Sending your feedback and problems”](17-troubleshooting.md#ch17-feedback)).
