# Glossary and contact {#ch19-title}

This chapter is a quick reference: the terms you'll meet in the guide and in Claude's messages, all the WhyNot Cut commands in one table, and how to contact us.

## Glossary {#ch19-glossary}

The terms are in alphabetical order by their English name, because that's the name you may see in files and messages. The Arabic term the Arabic guide uses is in parentheses after each one.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| AI buttons (أزرار الذكاء الاصطناعي) | In version 3.0: specific buttons in the WhyNot Cut panel that write a suggestion (a correction of the on-screen text, titles, or a summary), and nothing changes until you click the apply button ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-ai)). |
| Animatic (الأنيماتيك) | A simplified video and storyboard that show you how the motion video will move before it's built. |
| API key (مفتاح API) | In version 3.0: a secret code from an Anthropic developer account. You don't need one: the WhyNot Cut panel's buttons work as long as a Claude chat is open ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-ai)). |
| Apple Silicon (معالج Apple) | Apple's processors in newer Mac computers, such as Apple M1 or Apple M2. To find your computer's processor: the Apple menu, then About This Mac. WhyNot Cut works on them and on a Mac with an Intel processor ([Chapter 1](01-intro.md#ch01-requirements)). |
| Approval (الموافقة) | Your approval of the plan. No project is built without it. |
| Assets (ملفات الهوية) | The contents of `assets/`: the logo, fonts, photos and the colours file. WhyNot Cut never changes them. |
| B-roll (اللقطات المساعدة) | Footage shown over the speech or instead of the speaker's picture to illustrate the idea. |
| Backup copy (النسخة الاحتياطية) | In version 3.0: a copy saved before any change; Tidy saves it from the project file, Hush from the sequence, and Kalam, in the three-tools button, before it adds a captions track or MOGRT clips. Its name contains (WhyNot Tidy backup …), (WhyNot Hush backup …) or (WhyNot Kalam backup …), and undo brings it back ([Chapter 11](11-tidy.md#ch11-undo), [Chapter 12](12-hush.md#ch12-undo) and [Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-flow)). |
| Bridge (لوحة WhyNot Cut Bridge) | A small panel inside Premiere Pro and inside After Effects through which Claude builds the project, from Window › Extensions. What changes about it in version 3.0 is explained in [Chapter 1](01-intro.md#ch01-panels). |
| Captions (الكلام المكتوب على الشاشة / الكابشن) | On the video: the words written on screen. In publishing: the text of the post on the platform. |
| Chapters (فصول YouTube) | The parts of the episode with their timings in the YouTube description, taken from the topic map. |
| Claude badge (شارة Claude) | In version 3.0: a mark in the header of the WhyNot Cut panel that shows the status of the Claude app on your computer, with a button when you need to do something ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-header)). |
| Client folder (فولدر العميل) | One client's folder, with three folders inside: `assets/`, `data/`, `output/`. |
| Client profile (الملف التعريفي للعميل) | The client's brand, style, and the names and titles of their people, which every video relies on. |
| Contact sheet (الصورة المجمّعة) | One image that gathers all the graphics or all the previews so you can review them at a glance. |
| Content type (نوع المحتوى) | The reel's category, such as a story, advice or a success story, which sets its pacing recipe. |
| DMG (ملف `.dmg`) | A file on a Mac that you double-click, and the app appears inside it. You only meet it with the internal WhyNot Cut Setup program ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-setup-app)). |
| Engine (المحرك) | The program that does WhyNot Cut's work on your computer in the background. Today the start wizard installs it with your approval; in version 3.0 the install sets it up automatically ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-title)). You don't deal with it directly. |
| Episode (الحلقة) | A complete, finished podcast episode in `data/Episodes/`. |
| Episode sheet (ورقة الحلقة) | An `.md` file next to the episode with the show, the guest, the links, the sponsor, and the topics to avoid cutting (`avoid`). |
| Faithfulness check (حماية كلام المتحدث) | In version 3.0: in Kalam, every line Claude corrects is compared with what was said. If Claude shortened, reworded, translated or added to it, the original stays ([Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-faithful)). |
| Filler words (كلمات التردد) | “اممم” and “آه” or um and the like. In version 3.0: Hush removes them along with silences ([Chapter 12](12-hush.md#ch12-fillers)). |
| Graphics (الجرافيك) | The statements, cards and lists designed in the client's brand over the video. |
| Handoff (التسليم) | Files that explain what was done, what's left and where everything is, for you or for another computer. |
| Hook (البداية الجاذبة) | The first words of the video, which decide whether the viewer stays or leaves. |
| Kalam pack (حزمة «كلام») | In version 3.0: the caption models (Arabic dialects, English, and timing). The install sets them up automatically with the rest of WhyNot Cut, and you find them with their size on the “Models” page in the panel ([Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-pack)). |
| Log drawer (السجل) | In version 3.0: a folded drawer at the bottom of the WhyNot Cut panel with the technical details, and from it “Copy diagnostics” for support ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-errors)). |
| Markdown (.md) (ملف `.md`) | An ordinary text file, such as `HANDOFF.md`. Open it in TextEdit on a Mac or Notepad on Windows, or ask Claude to show it to you. |
| Marketplace (فولدر WhyNot Cut) | Today: the `whynot-cut-marketplace` folder our team sends, from which you add WhyNot Cut to the Claude app. Leave it in Documents and don't move it ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-first-run)). |
| MOGRT (قالب MOGRT) | A motion graphics template in Premiere (a `.mogrt` file). In version 3.0: Kalam puts the captions into it, one clip per caption ([Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-mogrt)). |
| Motion mode (وضع الموشن) | A motion graphics video made from the audio of part of an episode, built in After Effects. |
| Needs you (مطلوب منك) | A section in every report with what's waiting for your decision or your work. In version 3.0 you open it in the WhyNot Cut panel from the “Needs you” badge that shows how many items it has ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-header)). |
| Not verified (لم يُتحقَّق منه) | A section in the report with what wasn't checked, and why. |
| Panel (لوحة WhyNot Cut) | In version 3.0: one panel called WhyNot Cut in Premiere Pro and another in After Effects, from which you follow the work and approve the plan. It replaces the WhyNot Cut Bridge panel ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-title)). |
| Path (المسار) | A file's address on your computer: the names of the folders that lead to it, in order, such as `/Users/sample/Documents/WhyNot Clients` on a Mac, or `D:\WhyNot Clients` on Windows. |
| Plan (الخطة) | All the editing decisions before the build; its summary is shown to you for approval. |
| PlayerDebugMode (إعداد `PlayerDebugMode`) | A setting on the computer that lets Adobe programs open the unsigned WhyNot Cut Bridge panel. Don't look for it yourself: Claude gives you the line that turns it on, and WhyNot Cut never changes it ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-run-command)). |
| Plugin (إضافة WhyNot Cut) | The part that teaches Claude WhyNot Cut's commands and how it works, which you add to the Claude app once ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-first-run)). What appears inside Premiere Pro and After Effects is called a “panel”. |
| Podcast mode (وضع البودكاست) | Turning a whole episode into a reels plan, then reels in Premiere, then a publish package. |
| Podcast sync (سنك البودكاست) | In version 3.0: a new sequence that Tidy builds, in which the cameras and microphones are synced by sound ([Chapter 11](11-tidy.md#ch11-sync)); it's a normal sequence, not a Premiere multicam sequence. |
| Preferences (الإعدادات) | How WhyNot Cut works with you, saved in the `whynot-cut.local.md` file. |
| Preview video (فيديو المعاينة) | A quick MP4 video of the planned edit, for review only and not the final version. |
| Publish package (حزمة النشر) | The titles, description, chapters, keywords, captions and thumbnails. Nothing from it is uploaded. |
| QC (quality check) (فحص الجودة) | An independent check of frames that Premiere Pro or After Effects itself exports. |
| Raw footage (الخام) | The videos as they were filmed, in `data/Raw/`. |
| Recipe (وصفة نوع المحتوى) | Each type's pacing: silences, punch-ins, graphics, effects, and the feel of the music. |
| Reel (ريل (ريلز)) | A short vertical 9:16 video. |
| `reel` (أوامر المحرك) | The name of the engine's tool that Claude runs in the background. You see it in Claude's messages, and you don't type it. |
| Reels plan (خطة الريلز) | The reels suggested from the episode with timecode, type and score, for you to choose from. |
| Score (التقييم) | A suggested reel's grade: the strength of its opening, whether it makes sense on its own, its value, and the strength of its ending. |
| Script (السكريبت) | A file that builds the motion project inside After Effects when you run it from File › Scripts › Run Script File… ([Chapter 9](09-motion.md#ch09-ae)). |
| Sequence settings (إعدادات التسلسل) | The sequence's size in Premiere, its frame rate and its other settings, according to the client's settings. |
| Setup checklist (قائمة الإعداد) | In version 3.0: four items that confirm the computer is ready (Engine, Premiere link, Claude, Clients folder), and each missing item has a button that fixes it ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-checklist)). |
| Silence threshold (حد الصمت) | In version 3.0: in Hush, the sound level below which counts as silence. It's worked out for each clip in “Simple”, and you type it yourself in “Advanced” ([Chapter 12](12-hush.md#ch12-strength)). |
| Slash command (أمر يبدأ بالعلامة `/`) | A command you type in Claude's message box, such as `/whynot-cut:edit`, then Enter. All the commands are in the next table ([All commands on one page](#ch19-commands)). |
| SRT (ملف SRT) | A text file for captions, with every caption and its timing. In version 3.0: Kalam writes one with every run ([Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-outputs)). |
| Terminal / PowerShell (نافذة الأوامر) | A text window that comes with the operating system: Terminal on a Mac, and on Windows it's called PowerShell. You paste a line from this guide or from Claude into it and press Enter to run it, and you type nothing else in it ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-run-command)). |
| Thumbnail (الصورة المصغرة) | The video's cover image on YouTube. |
| Timecode (التايم كود) | A time in hours, minutes and seconds, such as 00:12:04. |
| Topic map (خريطة الموضوعات) | The parts of the episode in order, each with a number such as T01 then T02. It is the same as the YouTube chapters. |
| Transcript (التفريغ النصي) | Everything that was said, written out, with the timing of each word. |
| UXP Plugins (قائمة UXP Plugins) | In version 3.0: the name of the menu in Premiere Pro from which you open the new WhyNot Cut panel, from Window › UXP Plugins. The menu is part of Premiere, and you don't need to install anything from it ([Chapter 1](01-intro.md#ch01-panels)). |
| Verified (تم التحقق) | A section in the report with what was actually checked, with the evidence for each item. |
| Whisper (نموذج التفريغ) | The transcription model that runs on your computer: `large-v3` is the most accurate, and the `large-v3-turbo` model is the fastest. |
| WhyNot Hush («هَش») | In version 3.0: a tool in the WhyNot Cut panel that removes silences and filler words from the sequence ([Chapter 12](12-hush.md#ch12-title)). |
| WhyNot Kalam («كلام») | In version 3.0: the captions tool in the WhyNot Cut panel: a captions track, a MOGRT template, or an SRT file ([Chapter 13](13-kalam.md#ch13-title)). |
| WhyNot Tidy («تايدي») | In version 3.0: a tool in the WhyNot Cut panel that organises the Premiere project and builds the podcast sync ([Chapter 11](11-tidy.md#ch11-title)). |
| XML (ملف XML) | A project file in a general format that Premiere imports from File › Import instead of a native Premiere project. You get it if Premiere isn't on the computer ([Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-first-run)). |

> **In 3.0 — not verified yet:** The rows that mention version 3.0 describe what hasn't reached you yet, and their wording may change slightly with the final panel.

> **Tip:** In the internal logs you may come across the names Gate 0, Gate 1, Gate 2. In order, they are: your pick of the reels, your approval of the plan, and the quality-check verdict that Claude records after looking at the evidence itself. You don't need these names in your work.

## All commands on one page {#ch19-commands}

You can type each command as it is, or say what you want in your own words, like the phrases in the middle column.

| Command | Or say | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/whynot-cut:start` | “إزاي أبدأ” (“How do I start?”) | First-time setup step by step, until you put in your first video. |
| `/whynot-cut:autopilot` | “عدّل الفيديوهات الجديدة” (“Edit the new videos”) | Edits all the new videos, with one combined approval. |
| `/whynot-cut:edit C0001` | “عدّل الفيديو C0001” (“Edit video C0001”) | A full edit of one video ([Chapter 6](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-title)). |
| `/whynot-cut:podcast EP01` | “طلّع ريلز من الحلقة” (“Make reels from the episode”) | A reels plan from the episode, then you choose, then reels in Premiere and a publish package ([Chapter 8](08-podcast.md#ch08-title)). |
| `/whynot-cut:motion EP01 R03` | “اعمل موشن من الجزء ده” (“Make a motion video from this part”) | A motion video from the audio of part of the episode, in After Effects ([Chapter 9](09-motion.md#ch09-title)). |
| `/whynot-cut:preview` | “ورّيني الفيديو” (“Show me the video”) | An MP4 preview video of the planned edit, without Premiere. |
| `/whynot-cut:revise "1- … 2- …" C0001` | “ملاحظات على الفيديو” (“Notes on the video”) | Carries out your notes, rebuilds and re-checks, and writes a report on each note ([Chapter 10](10-revisions.md#ch10-title)). |
| `/whynot-cut:status` | “جاهز؟” (“Ready?”) | Where the video has got to, with fresh evidence and without changing anything. |
| `/whynot-cut:fix-audio` | “شيل السكتات” (“Remove the silences”) (for a clip before the build) | Cleans the dialogue audio and removes silences only, for a clip still in the editing stages before the build. The sequence open in Premiere has Hush in version 3.0 ([Chapter 12](12-hush.md#ch12-chat)). |
| `/whynot-cut:new-client` | “عميل جديد” (“New client”) | The client profile for a new client, from their brand files and reference videos. |
| `/whynot-cut:handoff` | “سلّم الشغل” (“Hand off the work”) | The handoff files, for you or for another computer. |
| `/whynot-cut:preferences` | “كلمني بالعربي” (“Talk to me in Arabic”) | Approval, language, transcription model and clients folder ([Chapter 16](16-preferences.md#ch16-title)). |
| `/whynot-cut:setup` | “نقلت على جهاز جديد” (“I've moved to a new computer”) | Installs or repairs the engine and the WhyNot Cut Bridge panel. |
| `/whynot-cut:help` | “ايه أوامر WhyNot Cut” (“What are the WhyNot Cut commands?”) | A short sheet of the commands, highlighting the ones that fit your situation right now. |
| `/whynot-cut:adobe-extras` | “اعملها في أفتر إفكتس” (“Do it in After Effects”) | An After Effects or Photoshop version of a video, only when you ask for it. |
