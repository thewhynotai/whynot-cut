# Quick tour {#ch03-title}

In version 3.0: six steps from an empty computer to your first Premiere Pro project. Each step is explained in detail in its own chapter; here you see them once from start to finish, with a picture of what you will see at each step.

> **Tip:** Today: instead of steps 2 to 5, follow the section [“Today: installing with the start wizard”](02-install.md#ch02-first-run) in Chapter 2, then go to step 6. The rest of this chapter describes version 3.0.

## From installation to your first project in 6 steps {#ch03-six}

> **In 3.0 — not verified yet:** The pictures in this tour will be taken from a full real run when version 3.0 is released.

1. **Get the Claude app.** Download it from claude.ai/download and install it, then sign in with your account ([First time in the Claude app](02-install.md#ch02-claude-first)).

   ![Step 1: the Claude app is ready and your account is signed in.](shots/03-quick-tour-1.png)

   *Step 1: the Claude app is ready and your account is signed in.*
2. **Paste the install line, or ask Claude.** Copy the install line for your computer from Chapter 2 or from the download page, and paste it into Terminal on a Mac or into PowerShell on Windows, then press Enter. Or add WhyNot Cut in the Claude app and type “ثبّت WhyNot Cut” (“Install WhyNot Cut”). Both paths are in [Chapter 2](02-install.md#ch02-one-line) and [“From inside Claude”](02-install.md#ch02-from-claude). On a Mac, macOS asks you for some permissions the first time ([macOS permissions](02-install.md#ch02-mac-permissions)).

   ![Step 2: one line starts the installation.](shots/03-quick-tour-2.png)

   *Step 2: one line starts the installation.*
3. **Everything is installed, and the checklist completes.** Don't press anything during the countdown, so the default answers are used. If you want to choose for yourself, press C to customise. The steps appear line after line, and the large models keep downloading in the background. Your personal preferences, such as approving every plan and what name to call you, are asked by the start wizard afterwards in Claude ([Chapter 16](16-preferences.md#ch16-title)).

   ![Step 3: the checklist is complete, and the models are downloading in the background.](shots/03-quick-tour-3.png)

   *Step 3: the checklist is complete, and the models are downloading in the background.*
4. **Premiere Pro and After Effects restart once.** This happens automatically if they have no unsaved work; otherwise you are asked to save your work, then click the restart button. After that you find the WhyNot Cut panel in Premiere from Window › UXP Plugins, and in After Effects from Window › Extensions, and the old panels, such as WhyNot Cut Bridge, and the test panels are removed. If you open the panel before your computer's setup is complete, it shows the “Setup checklist” ([Chapter 7](07-premiere-panel.md#ch07-checklist)).

   ![Step 4: the panel in Premiere after the restart.](shots/03-quick-tour-4.png)

   *Step 4: the panel in Premiere after the restart.*
5. **Claude opens on the start wizard.** If it did not open by itself at the end of the installation, open Claude and a new chat, type `/whynot-cut:start` and press Enter. The wizard asks about your preferences and your first client, creates the first client folder, then shows you where each file goes in it ([Chapter 4](04-client-folder.md#ch04-title)).

   ![Step 5: the first client folder, ready for you to add the files.](shots/03-quick-tour-5.png)

   *Step 5: the first client folder, ready for you to add the files.*
6. **“Drop the files · Say what you want · Approve the plan”.** Put the logo, fonts, photos and raw videos in the folders Claude shows you, then type “عدّل الفيديوهات الجديدة” (“Edit the new videos”). Approve the plan in the chat (and in version 3.0 from the Premiere panel too). You get a Premiere project, the preview video and the QC (quality check) report. The details are in [Chapter 6](06-edit-one-video.md#ch06-title).

   ![Step 6: the first Premiere project for Example Clinic.](shots/03-quick-tour-6.png)

   *Step 6: the first Premiere project for Example Clinic.*
