# Support

## How to reach us

Message us on WhatsApp with the WhatsApp button on this page. It is The Why Not AI's support line.

If you prefer email: info@thewhynot.ai

We reply within one working day (Sun–Thu, Cairo time).

## What to send

1. In the WhyNot Cut panel, open the “Log” at the bottom, then click “Copy diagnostics”. The panel says “Copied.” It copies a short summary of recent activity in plain words. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it yourself.
2. Paste the details into your WhatsApp message or your email, and write what you were doing, what happened, what you expected to happen, and the clip or episode number if there is one, like C0001.

Coming in a later update, not in the panel yet: one button, “Copy details and open WhatsApp”. It does both, one after the other: it copies the details, then opens the support chat on WhatsApp for you, and you paste them and send. Your details are never put into the link. Until then, use “Copy diagnostics” as above.

If you don't have the panel yet: ask Claude to sum up what it tried and what it found, and send that summary with the check result on WhatsApp or to the same address.
