# Support

## How to reach us

Email us at info@thewhynot.ai

We reply within one working day (Sun–Thu, Cairo time).

## What to send

1. In the WhyNot Cut panel, open the “Log” at the bottom, then click “Copy diagnostics”. The panel says “Copied.” It copies a short summary of recent activity in plain words. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it yourself.
2. Paste the details into your email, and write what you were doing, what happened, what you expected to happen, and the clip or episode number if there is one, like C0001.

Coming in a later update, not in the panel yet: one button that copies the details and opens this page for you. Your details will never be put into the page link. Until then, use “Copy diagnostics” as above.

If you don't have the panel yet: ask Claude to sum up what it tried and what it found, and send that summary with the check result to the same address.
