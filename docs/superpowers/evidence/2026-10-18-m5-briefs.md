## The estimator (Q58-2): calibrated on the subscription, provisional
**Status: provisional: re-check with count_tokens when the API resumes (Nov).** The API lane login was not used.

Method (a controlled measurement read back from the session's own usage numbers):
1. Six synthetic samples (~3,000 characters each, own text, no client material) were printed one per tool call in a
   Claude Code session on the subscription (model claude-opus-5-5): Arabic without marks, Arabic with full marks,
   English, digits, JSON-like punctuation, and a brief-shaped Arabic/English/timecode mix.
2. The session's own transcript JSONL was read for **key names and numbers only** (`message.id`, `usage.input_tokens`,
   `cache_creation_input_tokens`, `cache_read_input_tokens`, `output_tokens`; responses de-duplicated by message id;
   no message text). The added input of request n+1 = its prompt total − request n's prompt total − request n's
   output tokens.
3. Empty tool results measured the per-call overhead: 39 tokens, twice (of which the "no output" notice is about 8),
   so overhead = 31. Two samples were repeated: identical deltas (2049 and 880), so the reading is deterministic.

| Sample | Characters | Added input (measured) | Sample tokens (− 31) | Estimate | Estimate / measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ar_plain | 3,020 | 2,049 | 2,018 | 2,142 | 1.061 |
| ar_diacritics | 3,061 | 2,945 | 2,914 | 3,061 | 1.050 |
| en_plain | 3,020 | 880 | 849 | 903 | 1.064 |
| digits | 3,011 | 1,559 | 1,528 | 1,611 | 1.054 |
| json_punct | 3,020 | 2,052 | 2,021 | 2,125 | 1.051 |
| mixed | 3,061 | 1,730 | 1,699 | 1,744 | 1.026 |

**In-sample fit plus 3 held-out checks.** The fit is least squares over five classes (Arabic letter, Arabic mark,
Latin letter, digit, punctuation + newline; spaces free) on the six samples above, within ±2.3 % of each, then raised
5 %. That alone is an in-sample check: it cannot fail on new text. The first round (b2db1e7) said "+5 % so no sample is
undercounted" as if it were a safety margin; on the text it caps, it was not.

Held-out checks (fix round 1, review b2db1e7 P2-1), never part of the fit:

| Held-out sample | Measured | Measured by | Estimate, old rates | Estimate, pinned rates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `brief_largest_b2db1e7.txt`: the largest-fixture brief exactly as b2db1e7 + patches printed it | 1,587 | measured by the reviewer via subscription transcript usage, 2026-10-19 (reproduced here: 1,618 − 31) | 1,484 (−6.5 %) | 1,614 (+1.7 %) |
| `en_identifiers.txt`: synthetic path- and identifier-heavy English (3,061 chars) | 1,401 | this fix round, the same read-back, twice identical (1,432 − 31) | 1,287 (−8.1 %) | 1,450 (+3.5 %) |
| `brief_largest_fu1.txt`: the largest-fixture brief after this fix | 1,452 | this fix round, measured **after** the rates were pinned (a validation they were not tuned on), twice identical (1,483 − 31) | n/a | 1,483 (+2.1 %) |

The first two showed identifier-heavy English undercounted by 6-8 %, so the Latin rate was raised 0.333 → 0.41 and the
digit rate 0.621 → 0.645 (the smallest round raise that leaves both with ≥ 1.5 % to spare; the in-sample fit itself
was not redone, there is no identifier-shaped sample in it). The third was measured after that and holds: the brief
the lead now gets is **1,452 real tokens** under the 1,500 cap. Cost: fluent English prose is now overcounted about
29 % (`en_plain`: 1,092 for 849), the safe direction for a cap; every other sample stays within +10 %.

Pinned rates (tokens per character): Arabic letter 0.844, Arabic mark 1.415, Latin letter 0.41, digit 0.645,
punctuation/newline 0.919, space 0, other 2.0. "Other" (emoji, ZWJ/ZWNJ and other format characters, accented Latin,
other scripts) was not measured: 2.0 is a conservative stand-in. The samples and measured counts are
`tests/fixtures/brief/calibration/` (`calibration.json`: `samples` = the fit, `held_out` = the checks).
`test_c12_calibration_samples_within_tolerance` keeps every fit sample within [measured, measured × 1.10] (`en_plain`:
× 1.30, with its reason); `test_c12_held_out_samples_never_undercounted` and
`test_b6_largest_brief_estimate_at_least_its_measured_count` keep every held-out sample at or above its measured count.

Limits of this calibration: one model's tokenizer as the subscription reports it; ~3k-character samples; the overhead
subtraction carries about ±8 tokens. Re-check with `count_tokens` in November (on the list).

### The client brief (Task 59) under the pinned rates
The client brief (lib/client_brief.py, cap 1,000) uses the same estimator. Its largest fixture as 9835f7f printed it
was estimated at 1,000 with the old rates and measured **1,049** real tokens (this fix round, same read-back: 1,080 − 31),
so it was over its cap too. With the pinned rates the old layout no longer fit without cutting people and the rules
that forbid something, which are protected. Fix round 1 made it fit without cutting them: the cuts of the trimmable
parts are said once, in a closing `cut to fit, in output/client_database/: …` line (one shared folder instead of five
full paths), a label or section whose every line is cut is not printed, and the Arabic half of the unknown-gender note
is «غير معروف، اسأل المونتير». Result: estimate 998, measured **978** real tokens, with all 14 people and all 6 rules
that forbid something. When even the never-cut lines exceed the cap, the first line now says "over the cap".

## Short JSON (Q58-1): the six top sinks
Synthetic representative documents (the ones in `tests/test_brief_cli.py`); real sizes vary with the clip.

| Verb | Full | Short (incl. `full` + `summary`) | Dropped keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| previews | 49 | 49 | none (already short) |
| plan | 80 | 80 | none (every key is read somewhere) |
| ppro-build | 339 | 171 | bridge, result, at, job_data, content_signature, prproj_mtime, closed_previous, previous_restored, closed_by_pipeline_at |
| transcript-correct | 280 | 185 | meta |
| audio-clean | 3,605 | 452 | regions_ms (`metrics` is kept since fix round 1: the speech-to-noise and spectrogram proof) |
| qc | 61 | 61 | none |

How it reads: M5.2 found these stages expensive because the lead's context is re-read every loop, not because of their
output; the short form trims what each loop adds (biggest on audio-clean and ppro-build). The key inventory
(`test_f23_key_inventory_every_key_read_is_in_the_short_form`) scans skills, agents, hooks, `serve/**`, the panel
sources and the tests for every key of the verb's full document within six lines of the verb: every hit is in the
short form, except two hand-checked false hits recorded in the test (a fake bridge response's `result`, the
plan-approval record's `at`), which must stay hits. Every other verb: "not yet" (`brief.NOT_YET`).

The lexical scan cannot see a read by meaning ("speech-to-noise before and after" in the audio engineer's text needs
`metrics`), and the first round dropped `metrics` that way. Since fix round 1, `SEMANTIC_READS` in `tests/test_brief.py`
is a hand list of agent and skill phrases with the key each one needs (24 rows over audio-clean, ppro-build and
transcript-correct, from `agents/*.md` and `skills/**`); each phrase must still be in its file and its key in the short
form. `DROPPED_BY_MEANING` gives every dropped key its hand-checked reason, so a newly dropped key fails until someone
reads the texts for it.
