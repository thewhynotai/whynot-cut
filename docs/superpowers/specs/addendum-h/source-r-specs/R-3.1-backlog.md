# 3.1 backlog behaviours (not built in 3.0)

So 3.1 needs no evidence report, the remaining Source-R behaviours are recorded here, name-free, with our direction.
Each gets a full "Our version" block (the seven R2 points) when 3.1 planning starts. The shared scene contract of
R-6.2 applies to every scene.

## Rules adopted now (owners named)
- **Revisions keep the original** (owner: executor, `revise` + `handoff`): an audio-only or a picture-segment revision
  makes a **new version**, keeps the original untouched, and re-runs every delivery gate (R-4.5 encode, safe zones,
  R-6.4 keep-out, loudness) on the new version before it can be handed off.
- **Delivery-encode rules per platform**: owned by R-4.5 (the `encode` block + `encode_findings`).
- **Presenter-motion gate**: owned by R-6.4 (the presenter-motion check).

## Other 3.1 items
- **LOOK-04, 05, 08, 09, 11, 12** (blocks in R-6.1).
- **Screen-recording scene**: camera keys `{at, focus rect, zoom, move}` (a zero-length move = a cut), zoom refused
  above a set maximum, click ripples at normalised pointer positions, pinned callouts (rect/circle) inside the safe
  zone, a fixed screen area in the frame. Ours: Premiere Transform keyframes (eased, T4.1) or AE; our own maximum zoom
  chosen by a readability measurement on real screen recordings (basis recorded); the screen area is a layout slot of
  the look (R-6.1); callouts checked by R-4.5.
- **Privacy redaction**: the assistant proposes rectangles and time ranges from temporary stills, the editor confirms,
  then a new plate with pixelation or blur plus verification frames and a needs-review state. Ours: an engine pass
  (FFmpeg pixelize/boxblur with `enable='between(t,a,b)'`, NVENC) or native Premiere Mosaic/Gaussian Blur with
  tracked masks (editable); proposal frames go through the frame-inspector.
- **Custom scenes**: Source-R lets the assistant author free-form scene code. Ours: **no free code**; a JSON
  custom-scene spec composed from our scene primitives, with editable fields as Essential Graphics properties.
- **No-recording motion videos**: an authored spec with no recording, a generated silent base, full-takeover scenes,
  never a fake transcript. Ours: authored copy with reading-time timing in AE.
- **Chunked preview cache**: renders longer than a threshold are split into GOP-rounded chunks with a cache identity (comp, props,
  assets, encoding), decode and black-frame checks per chunk. Ours: for `preview_video.py` with NVENC, so a revision
  re-renders only changed ranges; our chunk threshold is chosen by measurement on our previews (basis recorded).
- **Podcast verified-reuse resume**: reuse a clip only when its inputs, options, media bytes, output and QC all match;
  each clip has its own render-options file. Ours: for `podcast_cli.py` batch runs, with a per-reel options file next
  to its plan; selection quality stays ours (episode-analyst, moment-scouts, Gate 0).
- **Reference-style evidence**: references are recorded with sha256, duration and fps; every observed trait gets an
  evidence id with a timecode and a confidence; decisions are split into "keep" and "adapt", stored per project, and
  never become a new global look. Ours: in `ref_style.py` (client-level `editing-style.json`), with evidence ids and
  timecodes the client-researcher cites.
- **In-place forms for every scene type** in the Review tab (R-6.5).
