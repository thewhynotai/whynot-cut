# R-4.7 Punch-in emphasis scoring (eased, face-safe) + endings order

Phase 4, Task 29 (with Task 1, smooth zoom). Owner: executor (`plan.py` punch logic, endings).

## Source-R behaviour

**Emphasis punches**
- A per-word score from loudness compared with the neighbouring words and with the speech median, boosted for
  numbers, product names and the call-to-action keyword.
- Proposed punch-ins scale the picture slightly, a few seconds apart (the spacing follows the pace).
- Without a measured head envelope a punch is marked as needing review; punches are never applied automatically.

**Endings**
- Up to three ending parts, only those switched on, always in this order: a comment call-to-action keyword, a follow
  card (the full handle, up to a length limit), then the recorded sign-off.
- The recorded sign-off is never cut and never covered by a graphic. The call-to-action default comes from the
  profile and can be turned off per video.

## Our version

### Emphasis score (one signal for the story-editor, not an auto-edit)
- Per word: `0.6·loudness_z` (RMS vs the 8 surrounding words) `+ 0.2·after_pause` (a pause ≥ 300 ms before the
  word) `+ boosts` (no pitch signal: the engine has no pitch tracker): a spoken number +0.3, a client name/product
  from the client database +0.2, the CTA keyword +0.2, a word the story-editor marked as the statement's key word +0.3.
- Candidates: top-scoring words that start a phrase, at least `min_gap_s` apart (recipe: 6 s for calm types, 4 s for
  energetic), never inside the first 1.5 s (the hook has its own treatment) or the ending.
- Each punch, one formula: `s_max` = the largest scale ≤ 1.12 that keeps the measured head box plus a 6 % margin
  inside the punched frame; if `s_max < 1.05` the punch is dropped (it would not read as a punch); otherwise
  `scale = 1.05 + (s_max − 1.05) × r`, where `r` (0..1) is the word's score rank among this reel's candidates
  (1 = highest). 1.05, 1.12 and 6 % are starting values of ours, measured on our reels (basis recorded); the keys are **eased** keyframes (T4.1 smooth zoom: ease in 8 frames, hold,
  ease out 10 frames at 30 fps), centred on the measured face so **the face stays fully inside the punched frame**
  with a 6 % margin (shots/face data); no face data → the punch is centred on the window and marked for review.
- Output: candidates with score and a plain reason in the edit-decisions draft; the story-editor keeps or drops them;
  the plan gate approves.

### Endings
- Order fixed as above, per client profile: `endings: {cta: {on, keyword_ar, keyword_en}, follow: {on, handle,
  logo}, sign_off: {on, phrase}}`.
- The CTA wording comes from the client voice (Arabic or English); the follow card uses the client logo from the
  client database and our look (R-6.1); the handle is always shown in full: it is fit-or-fail measured and shrunk
  down to the look's label floor; only a handle that still does not fit is refused by the plan, with the fix.
- A **sign-off detector** runs on our transcript (normalised match of the profile phrase in the last 20 s); the
  detected span is protected from cuts (R-4.3), graphics, captions and music ducking changes; nothing is placed over
  it. Not found when expected → one line to the editor, the last 8 s are protected.

## Acceptance
Fixture tests: punches at least `min_gap_s` apart, scale within 1.05–1.12 and never cropping the head box, eased keys, face inside the punched frame
on shot fixtures; no punch in the hook or the ending; endings always in order; the sign-off is never cut or covered;
a live check on a whynot-test clip in Premiere.
