# R-6.1 Look system: one named motion law per look, brand-aware for all looks, contrast stepping

Phase 6 (R-T2 per look). 3.0 builds the system + LOOK-01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 10; 3.1 builds LOOK-04, 05, 08, 09, 11, 12
(their "Our version" blocks are here so 3.1 needs no evidence). Owners: looks track (look data in
`config/defaults/looks/`, `wnc_motion_lib.jsx`, `lib/looks*.py`), executor (`motion_layout.py`, `motion_plan.py`
look selection), Adobe (panel picker).

## Source-R behaviour (the system)

- Twelve visual styles. A style changes how every shared scene looks (palette, default font, caption presentation,
  card shapes, accents, composition and motion character); only one style brings scenes of its own.
- Each style says whether a picture-in-picture presenter is allowed, which purposes and tones it suits, and which kinds
  of scene make it a better match.
- Three motion characters are shared by all styles: a settle-down entrance, a staggered entrance and a quiet one. The
  newest style uses **one entrance curve for every word**: each word rises slowly over one long, fixed duration with no blur,
  scale or overshoot; scenes slide in and out as rigid slabs with hard cuts between them.
- Only 2 of the 12 styles take the creator's brand colours; the other 10 use their own palettes.
- Brand colours are adapted: the background is kept, then ink and highlight lightness are stepped until every text
  pair reaches 4.5:1; otherwise the style's own colours are used; a notice names what changed. Colours paint graphics
  only, never the presenter.
- Scenes in an edit must be ordered and non-overlapping; a scene that belongs to one style is refused in the others.
- The creator calls 2 styles "proven"; the other 10 need a preview gate before delivery.
- The style table in the docs is generated from the style definitions.

## Our version (the system)

### A look is our own data model: layout slots + palette roles + type scale + surfaces + marks + one motion law
Files (all under the looks track's `config/defaults/looks/`):
- `config/defaults/looks/motion/LOOK-NN.json` — one file per motion look (the schema test loads only this folder);
- `config/defaults/looks/caption-looks.json` — the caption looks (R-4.1);
- `config/defaults/looks/recommender.json` — **the only home** of fit tables and trait vectors (R-4.6);
- `config/defaults/looks/sfx-palettes.json` — SFX palettes (R-6.7);
- `config/defaults/looks/motion-laws.json` — the named motion laws (below).

```json
{"code": "LOOK-01", "display_name": "LOOK-01", "status": "preview_gate",
 "layout": {"presenter": "full_bleed",
            "slots": {"main": {"anchor": "face_opposite", "box": [0.06, 0.16, 0.62, 0.58]},
                      "alt":  {"anchor": "lower", "box": [0.06, 0.60, 0.94, 0.78]}},
            "mirror_rtl": true},
 "palette_roles": {"paper": "brand.background|neutral.light", "ink": "brand.text|neutral.ink",
                   "accent": "brand.primary", "highlight": "brand.accent", "muted": "derived:ink@40%"},
 "type_scale": {"display": {"role": "title", "weight": 700, "px": [76, 104]},
                "body": {"role": "body", "weight": 500, "px": [44, 56]},
                "label": {"role": "caption", "weight": 600, "px": [32, 38]}},
 "surfaces": {"card": {"radius": 22, "fill": "paper", "inner_frame_px": 2, "frame": "accent@60%", "shadow": "soft"}},
 "marks": {"emphasis": "colour", "divider": "none"},
 "caption_look": "phrase-boxed", "caption_motion": "full",
 "motion_law": "ML-DOCK", "sfx_palette": "SFX-CALM", "playful": false,
 "exclusive_scenes": []}
```
- **Layout slots** are fractions of the safe-text rectangle of the selected safe-zone profile (R-4.5), so one look
  works on every platform and frame size. `anchor` rules: `face_opposite` (the slot moves to the side away from the
  head envelope, R-6.4), `upper`, `lower`, `centre`, `full` (takeover). `mirror_rtl` flips horizontal anchors for
  Arabic.
- **Slot resolution order** (deterministic): (1) the head envelope decides first: a `face_opposite` slot goes to the
  side whose slot box does not overlap the envelope; (2) only when **both** sides are free (no face, or a small face)
  does `mirror_rtl` decide (the reading-start side: right for Arabic, left for English); (3) when **neither** side is
  free (a centred or close-up face, common in vertical reels), the engine first tries to **shift the presenter crop**
  sideways within the window limits (our per-shot framing, the same limits as R-6.4's crop fix) until one side's slot
  is free; (4) only then the look's `alt` slot is used; (5) if `alt` also overlaps the envelope, the plan gets a
  keep-out finding with the R-6.4 fix proposals. A reel with no presenter (R-6.4 EXEMPT) uses step 2 directly.
- **Slot vs caption band**: a slot never overlaps the safe-zone profile's `caption_band` while captions show. If it
  would, the caption is moved by the existing avoid logic (`previews.render_captions` `avoid`) when there is room,
  else the slot shrinks toward its anchor down to 80 % of its height; still overlapping → captions are hidden during
  that scene (existing `captions_hidden_during` behaviour) with a plan note.
- **Palette roles** map to the client brand by default (all 12 looks are brand-aware); `neutral.*` is the look's own
  fallback. Never a hard-coded client colour.
- **Type scale** uses the client's font roles (`font_roles.py`); the house fallback is our D13 set (Syne / Inter / IBM
  Plex Sans Arabic). Arabic never uses italic; emphasis is weight or colour.
- `exclusive_scenes`: scenes only this look may use (e.g. LOOK-09's offer card); `motion_plan` refuses them under
  other looks. Scenes stay ordered and non-overlapping (existing plan validation).
- `caption_motion`: `"full"` (captions use their caption look's entrance and exit) or `"none"` (captions cut in and
  out with no animation and no moving highlight; static emphases stay; R-4.1).
- `status`: `planned` (specified, not built: never listed by the picker or the recommender, never loadable by a build)
  → `preview_gate` (built; the editor may pick it by hand; the recommender never auto-picks it) → `accepted` (passed
  the distinctiveness gate on 3 client brands). **3.0 ships only the 6 built look files**; the 3.1 looks stay
  `planned` in docs and arrive as files with 3.1 (G10: nothing half-built can be picked).
- A generated look table (a `docs/` page) is checked against the files by a test, so docs never drift.

### Motion laws (ours): one per look, stored as named AE easing presets (`motion-laws.json`)
Every scene graphic of a look (text, cards, marks, scene changes) uses its look's law; spoken-word captions are
not scene graphics and follow their caption look's motion (R-4.1). Frames are at 30 fps and scaled by
fps/30. Text never gets motion blur. Every move follows the **direction rule** (00-INDEX: progress from the reading
start to the reading end; arrivals from the reading end; listed exceptions).
When a look is active, its law decides both the curves and the scene-change move; an authored `transition_in` is
not used and `reel motion-plan` gives a plan warning naming the scene ("the look's own scene change is used"); the
recipe's ease (`smooth/snappy/bouncy/gentle`, `motion-beats.md`) only scales the law's durations
(snappy ×0.8, gentle ×1.3, others ×1.0).

| Law | Signature (entrance) | Stagger | Exit / scene change | Look |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ML-DOCK` | the card docks into its face-opposite slot: 28 px travel from the frame edge + opacity, 10 f, ease-out (influence 85 %, speed 0); the 2 px inner frame draws round after it, 8 f | 3 f | undocks back to the edge, 8 f | LOOK-01 |
| `ML-STEP` | mask wipe in reading direction, 10 f, ease in-out 60/60 | on each item's spoken `at` | 6 f fade | LOOK-02 |
| `ML-PUNCH` | per word at its spoken time: scale 112 % → 100 % + opacity, 8 f, ease-out 90 % | the speech | hold, then 6 f fade of the whole line | LOOK-03 |
| `ML-UNFOLD` | card unfolds from its top edge (scale Y 0 → 100 %), 14 f, ease-out 75 %; contents fade in from f 4 | 4 f | 8 f fold up | LOOK-04 |
| `ML-DRIFT` | 2.5D: Z 120 → 0 with the AE camera, 16 f, ease-out 70 %; rotation settles ±2° → 0 | 5 f | 10 f drift back | LOOK-05 |
| `ML-BALANCE` | two columns from opposite edges, 12 f, ease-out 80 %; the verdict bar draws by trim path, 10 f | 6 f between columns | 8 f fade | LOOK-06 |
| `ML-NOTE` | the keyword's underline draws from its reading start, 8 f; a margin note card slides 12 px along a thin leader line, 10 f, ease-out 70 % | on each keyword's spoken time | 6 f fade; the band stays | LOOK-07 |
| `ML-FROST` | panel rises 24 px, 12 f, ease-out 80 %, while its blur-behind fades in; a lens moves ease in-out 50/50 | 4 f | 8 f fade + blur out | LOOK-08 |
| `ML-STAMP` | ticket slides in 10 f with a 104 % → 100 % settle; the stub stamps (scale 120 % → 100 %, 5 f) | 6 f | 8 f slide out | LOOK-09 |
| `ML-SEAT` | the name chip grows out of the speaker's seat side (scale X 0 → 100 % from that edge), 9 f; the pull-quote band comes in over 12 f (AE: it dims and blurs the speaker frame behind it; Premiere overlay: a solid brand-paper band at 88 %, no blur); quote lines fade up 6 px, 8 f | 5 f per line | the band clears, 10 f | LOOK-10 |
| `ML-TRACE` | the route path trim-draws at constant speed between stops; each stop scales 90 % → 100 %, 6 f, ease-out | the path reaching each stop | 8 f fade | LOOK-11 |
| `ML-CROSS` | line-level mask reveal, 6 f, ease-out 65 %; scenes change by a 6 f cross-push of 40 px + opacity | 2 f per line | the cross-push | LOOK-12 |

These are deliberately unlike Source-R's: no single long rise for every word, no rigid-slab scene moves with hard
cuts, no shared three-character menu. Each law is tuned on real client reels before its look leaves `preview_gate`.

### Contrast stepping (all looks)
1. Keep `paper` (the background role) as the brand gives it.
2. For each text pair (ink on paper, ink on card, highlight text, label on accent), the needed ratio is
   `legibility.needed(size_px)`: today `min_contrast` 4.5:1, or `min_contrast_large` 3:1 at `large_px` 64 px and up
   (`motion.json`, profile-overridable). Specs and tests call `needed()`; they never hard-code a ratio.
3. Step the text colour's lightness in OKLCH (L ± 0.04 per step toward contrast, hue and chroma kept) up to 10 steps.
4. Still failing → the look's neutral for that role.
5. Measure again **over the pixels behind** the text on the rendered still (`legibility.legible`, G68), not only the
   swatch.
6. A notice per change in plain words (`"لون العنوان اتغمّق شوية عشان يتقري على خلفية البراند"`), shown at the plan
   gate. Colours paint graphics only, never the presenter.

### Better than Source-R (system level)
All looks brand-aware; one law per look (generalising the one-curve idea to every look); layout slots that follow
the face and the platform's safe zones; native AE layers and Premiere MOGRTs with Essential Graphics properties the
editor can still change; RTL-mirrored layouts; head keep-out (R-6.4); captions synced to word timing (R-4.1); SFX
matched to motion (R-6.7); a recommender with plain reasons (R-4.6); live stills on the editor's own clip.

### Distinctiveness gate (every look, R2.7)
The brand-systems-designer compares our look stills (AR + EN, 3 client brands) with **Source-R's own preview of
the same look** (`S01`–`S12` in `WhyNot-Cut-3.0-Addendum/evidence/source-r-frames/`, staged by the plan session; images only, never copied into the repo; nothing
fetched online; Source-R code never run) and must answer "recognisably ours, clearly better". A "no" is a P1 defect. The verdict and the stills are recorded in the looks ledger; a look moves from
`preview_gate` to `accepted` only after a "yes". Our looks must also differ **from each other** (LOOK-02 numbered
steps vs LOOK-10 seat-anchored quotes, for example).
