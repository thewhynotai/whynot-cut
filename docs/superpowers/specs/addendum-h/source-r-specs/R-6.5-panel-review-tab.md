# R-6.5 Panel Review tab: lists → seek, follow mode, stale-save guard, history

Phase 6 (R-T3). Owner: Adobe track (panel), executor (`reel serve` events, plan patching). In-place forms for every
scene type are 3.1.

## Source-R behaviour (its live review editor)

- **Launch**: opens from the first prepared draft (before cuts are applied) and stays open; binds 127.0.0.1 with a
  per-run token, rejects a foreign Host or Origin, serves only its own files, writes only inside the project.
- **Follow mode**: shows the assistant's latest completed change (cuts, captions, scenes, motion, sound, style) in a
  banner, with a switch to pause and resume following. A focused field, unsaved edits or an open draft hold
  incoming changes. **A stale save cannot overwrite a newer assistant save.** A button to view the newer update keeps
  the user's local draft first (stored in the browser) and survives a reload.
- **Panels**: Summary (counts, problems); Captions (search, edit word text, move start/end by frames or ms, split,
  merge, delete, emphasis colour/weight/pulse, shift all, mark reviewed/draft, click to seek); Scenes (timing,
  layout, motion and a schema-generated form with live length limits); Cuts (trim, split, delete; later items ripple;
  captions stay on their source moment; affected items listed before confirming); Effects (SFX recipe and gain,
  camera keys and transitions at the playhead).
- **Timeline lanes** for cuts, scenes, captions, sounds, camera, transitions; drag, wheel zoom, undo/redo, History
  (restore is undoable), Notes (issues), Guides (safe zones).
- **Save** validates the whole manifest and writes atomically, byte-identical when unchanged; a history copy per
  changing save (a fixed number of copies).
- **Export**: a quick proxy preview with no gates, and a final export with all delivery gates (failures shown); a disk
  and RAM budget check first; one export at a time; cancel kills only its own process.
- Known limits: no SFX in the preview; first play slightly ahead of the graphics; drafts kept per port; one tab per
  file.

## Our version

**Premiere is the player and the timeline**, so our tab needs no timeline UI and no browser player.
1. **Lists that seek**: captions, scenes, cut proposals (R-4.3), issues (QC, keep-out, safe zones); a click seeks
   Premiere (`set_player_position`); the current item follows the playhead.
2. **Follow banner** fed by `reel serve` events (plan compiled, stills rendered, build done, QC done), with pause /
   resume; incoming changes are held while a field is focused or an edit is unsaved.
3. **Stale-save guard**: every save carries the plan version number + content hash it was based on; `reel serve`
   refuses a save based on an older version and returns both versions so the panel offers "keep mine / take the new
   one / merge text fields"; nothing is lost (the refused draft is kept in the panel's per-reel draft store under the
   engine home, not browser storage).
4. **History**: a snapshot per changing save (newest 20 and at most 200 MB per reel, whichever comes first: our
   starting values, measured on long projects; under `output/`), restore is itself a save (undoable).
5. **Byte-identical save** when nothing changed (the plan's "approved" stamp stays valid).
6. **3.0 edits**: caption word text and timing (ms/frames), mark reviewed, cut-proposal accept/keep, scene text for
   the 3.0 scene types via a schema-generated form with live fit-or-fail (HarfBuzz via `reel serve`); after a save,
   `reel serve` patches **only the affected graphic clip or MOGRT** in place (UXP `executeTransaction`, CEP fallback).
7. **Export dialog** queues Adobe Media Encoder with our QC gates (R-6.4, R-4.5 safe zones, the R-4.5 delivery-encode
   check, loudness) and shows the results.
   Before queueing it checks free disk space and refuses **only when the estimated output (bitrate × duration ×
   1.2) cannot fit** on the output drive; low memory (≥ 85 % in use) gives a **warning** and the export **waits in the
   queue** until memory frees, or the editor starts it anyway (G10: a 16 GB Mac with Premiere open sits there
   normally);
   allows **one export per reel at a time**, and a cancel stops only that export's own job (by its job id), never
   other renders.
8. AR/EN, RTL, our panel design tokens; one-line Egyptian Arabic notices.

### Better than Source-R
Native timeline and player (no drift between picture and graphics), edits land in the editor's own Premiere project, drafts survive
in the engine home (not a browser), multi-client, QC gates of AE and Premiere together.

## Acceptance
Editing a scene title in the panel updates a **Premiere overlay / MOGRT** clip in place in ≤ 2 s; an **AE motion
scene** edit queues a rebuild + render of that scene with visible progress (no time bound, never blocking the panel); a stale save is refused with the three-way
choice; history restore works and is undoable; lists seek Premiere; AR/EN, RTL.
