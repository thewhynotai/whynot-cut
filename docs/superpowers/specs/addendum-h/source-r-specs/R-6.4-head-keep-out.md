# R-6.4 Head keep-out gate in QC read-back (AE and Premiere)

Phase 6. Owners: executor (`qc.py`, `motion_qc.py`, `shots.py` face data), Adobe (read-back ops), looks track
(`safe_zones.violations(..., face=…)`, R-4.5).

## Source-R behaviour

- Face detection with two detectors (near faces and far faces), sampled several times per second on a reduced
  frame, with a minimum confidence. Tracking smooths the box, ignores tiny moves, limits how fast the crop may move,
  holds the last position briefly when the face is lost, and follows the dominant face when there are several.
  Several aspect ratios; HDR or alpha sources are refused.
- Delivery also checks the presenter's motion (the crop must not move too fast or jitter).
- A **head envelope**: a measured per-frame head box. Every graphic box is tested against it.
- Outcomes: PASS / FAIL / NEEDS-REVIEW / EXEMPT. FAIL can be fixed by moving the presenter inside the split window,
  then re-checked. Frames with no face become NEEDS-REVIEW, and the human reviewer's name is recorded.
- Face detection runs in a headless browser.

## Our version

### Data
- Face boxes come from our engine's existing detectors (`shots.face_boxes`: macOS Vision on Mac, OpenCV on
  Windows); **no browser**. MediaPipe Python (Apache-2.0) is considered only if a measurement on our fixtures shows a
  clear recall gain (turned heads, low light) **and** it fits the lean runtime locks (G13); it is a new dependency, so
  the default is none.
- **Face box → head box**: the detectors return faces, not heads (hair, forehead and chin are outside). The head box
  is the face box grown by **+45 % of the face height on top, +15 % below, +20 % of the face width on each side**:
  a **starting guess of ours** (no recorded origin), to be measured as in Acceptance before the gate blocks anything;
  the measured values and their basis go in config (`motion.json` `head_from_face`, through the executor).
- **Sampling (one source)**: the face samples `shots.py` already takes for framing; where a scene's span has fewer
  than 3 of them, extra frames are sampled at the same analysis size. The analysis size is chosen by measurement.
- **Head envelope** per scene: the union of the head boxes over the span, grown by a 4 % safety margin of the head
  height on each side, in sequence coordinates after our per-shot framing (`shots.frame_shots`).
- Speaker-aware: for two-speaker shots the envelope is per seat (speaker turns), and only the seats visible in that
  scene's shots count.

### Gate (QC read-back)
- Every text and graphic box from the AE read-back or the Premiere read-back (`motion_readback`, `qc.py`) is tested
  against the envelope and the safe-zone profile (`safe_zones.violations`).
- Outcomes:
  - **PASS**: no overlap.
  - **FAIL**: a text box overlaps the envelope by ≥ 2 % of the box area, or a graphic covers ≥ 10 % of the head.
  - **NEEDS-REVIEW**: on a reel that **has** a presenter, no face found on ≥ 30 % of a scene's samples, or detection
    confidence too low; the frames are listed for the editor. The editor confirms **once per reel** (one click covers
    every NEEDS-REVIEW scene of that reel); the confirmation is recorded with their name and time.
  - **EXEMPT**: full-screen takeover scenes and B-roll cards (`qc.FULL_SCREEN`), and **every scene of a reel with no
    presenter track**: audio-only motion videos (the voice-pulse layer), faceless reels (e.g. LOOK-12 list explainers),
    screen recordings and no-recording motion videos. "No presenter" is decided from the plan, never from a failed
    detection, so a faceless reel never waits on a human: a new timeline-spec / motion-plan field
    `presenter: "on_camera" | "none"` (added through the executor), set by the engine from the source kind
    (`--as motion` audio-only → `none`; screen recording → `none`) and editable by the editor at the plan gate;
    missing = `on_camera`.
  - **Advisory face probe** when `presenter: none`: a cheap check on the face samples `shots.py` already has (or 6
    sampled frames when there are none) looks for a face; found in ≥ 30 % of samples → one advisory line («يبدو إن
    فيه حد ظاهر في الفيديو؛ تحب نخليه on_camera عشان نحمي وشه؟»), never a blocker.
- **Accept overlap** (editor override): the editor may accept a FAIL for one scene with a short reason (e.g.
  "intentional: the card covers the mouth while the product is shown"); recorded with their name and time like a
  NEEDS-REVIEW confirmation; an accepted FAIL no longer blocks handoff and the QC record shows it as accepted.
- **Fix proposals** (never silent): move the graphic to the look's alternate slot (upper/lower), or shift the presenter
  crop within the window limits, then re-check; the chosen fix goes through the plan (approval), and the gate re-runs
  after the rebuild.
- Advisory inside the NLE (the editor still finishes there); **blocking for the handoff package** (R-6.4 FAIL or an
  unconfirmed NEEDS-REVIEW stops `handoff`).

### Presenter-motion check (advisory)
The presenter crop's motion (our per-shot framing, punch-ins, crop shifts from R-6.1's slot resolution) is checked
for speed and jitter: no crop move faster than a set share of the frame width per second, and no back-and-forth
reversal within one second. Findings are shown at QC (advisory in the NLE, listed in the handoff report). Limits are
starting values of ours, measured on our reels, stored with their basis.

### Budget
The face pass **reuses the face samples `shots.py` already computed for framing** (no second decode); the gate adds
≤ 5 s per reel on top (a starting budget, measured; over budget = a warning, never a block).

### Better than Source-R
Shot- and speaker-aware envelopes (podcasts with two seats), no browser runtime, Mac Vision path, the same gate for AE
and Premiere, combined with platform safe zones in one check, proposals through the plan gate.

## Acceptance
A seeded overlap fails; the proposed fix (slot move or crop shift) passes after re-check; on a presenter reel, frames
without a face are listed as NEEDS-REVIEW and one per-reel confirmation records a name; an audio-only motion reel, a
faceless reel and a screen recording are EXEMPT and reach handoff with no human step; darwin path tested with Vision
fakes. **Head-expansion measurement**: 200 labelled frames from at least 10 of our reels (at least 40 with turned
heads, 40 in low light), run through both detectors; the grown box must contain the hand-labelled head box in
≥ 95 % of frames with a mean over-cover (grown area ÷ labelled area) ≤ 1.6; the measured expansion replaces the
starting guess and is recorded with the fixture set and date. Tests also cover: **accept overlap** (a FAIL accepted
with a reason records name + time and no longer blocks handoff); the **advisory face probe** (`presenter: none` with a
face in ≥ 30 % of samples gives the one line and never blocks); the **presenter-motion check** (a seeded fast crop
move and a back-and-forth reversal are reported, a smooth move is not); and the **budget** (the gate adds ≤ 5 s per
reel on a fixture reel, measured; over budget = a warning).
