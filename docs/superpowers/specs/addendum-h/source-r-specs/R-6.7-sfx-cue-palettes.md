# R-6.7 SFX cue palettes per look, driven by markers

Phase 6. Owners: executor (`sfx.py`, motion audio), looks track (palette data in `config/defaults/looks/`),
Adobe (markers).

## Source-R behaviour

- All sound effects are **synthesised** from deterministic recipes (48 kHz, stereo, 16-bit), so there are no licence
  questions: a small set of deterministic voices and recipes.
- Each style maps **cue kinds** (scene transition, a stamp, a swap, a click, a count tick, a reveal, typing) to a
  recipe, a gain and a lead time (so the sound's peak lands on the visual cue).
- Cues come from scene data (e.g. checklist item frames become reveal cues). Cue kinds have a priority order when two
  collide; a maximum number of events per second; nothing plays over the sign-off.
- Each palette entry records a confidence and a basis; the default is "authored, not verified", reviewed at the plan
  gate.

## Our version

- **Cue kinds (ours)**: `scene_change`, `logo`, `hit`, `exchange` (one thing replaces another), `tick` (a list
  item), `tally` (a number arriving at its value), `appear` (an element enters), `keys` (word-by-word typing).
  **Collisions (our rule, not an order list)**: when two cues fall within 120 ms, a structural cue (`scene_change`,
  `logo`) always wins over a non-structural one; when the two structural cues collide, `logo` wins (the logo sting
  carries that scene change, so one sound marks both); otherwise the cue whose visual event is **bigger on screen** wins (the larger area that changes
  at that moment, from the scene builder's marker data), a tie keeps the earlier marker; the dropped cue is recorded in
  the plan. Basis: a sound should belong to the most visible action (our motion direction, `motion-beats.md`).
- **Palettes per look** (`config/defaults/looks/sfx-palettes.json`): `SFX-<name>` → for each cue kind: our synth kind
  from `sfx.RECIPES` (or a client-mapped file from `06_audio/sfx-map.json`), gain dB, `lead_ms` (the recipe's peak
  time), and `basis` (`authored` until verified by ear on real reels, then `verified` with the reel id).
- **Event-driven placement from markers**: AE scene builders write a marker per visual cue (item appearing, counter
  reaching its value, stamp, scene change); Premiere overlay builds write sequence markers; `motion_audio` / `sfx` place each
  sound at `marker − lead_ms`.
- Rules: at most 3 events per second; none in the protected sign-off span (R-4.7); a cue louder than −24 dBFS that
  peaks within ±80 ms of a spoken word's onset (words.json) is lowered by 6 dB instead of moved; every SFX file has provenance (synth
  recipe hash or the client file's sha256) in the build record.
- New synth recipes we add are our own (FFmpeg lavfi graphs), designed by ear; we never copy Source-R's recipe tables.

## Acceptance
Cue kinds land with their lead time (±1 frame) from markers; collision tests for the rule above: a structural cue
beats a non-structural one even when the other's on-screen change is bigger, `logo` beats `scene_change`, between two
non-structural cues the bigger on-screen change wins, and an equal-area tie keeps the earlier marker (the dropped cue
is in the plan); the rate cap holds; the onset rule lowers a loud cue near a word onset;
nothing over the sign-off; a provenance entry per SFX; palettes load and validate.
