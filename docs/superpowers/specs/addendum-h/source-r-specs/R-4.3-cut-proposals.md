# R-4.3 Cut proposals v2: retakes, Egyptian filler rules, Arabic normalisation, markers, never past the sign-off

Phase 4, Task 25 (engine part: lib + plan integration + markers). Owner: looks track (`lib/cut_proposals*.py`).
`reel hush` (tools track, Phase 6B) reuses this engine. Plan/markers hooks go through the executor.

## Source-R behaviour

Cut proposals are computed from the reviewed transcript and the audio, shown as a table, and applied **only when the
creator approves**:
- **Silence gaps** above a minimum length are proposed for removal, padded before and after speech; cut points snap
  within a small window; nothing shorter than a minimum piece is removed or kept.
- **Retakes**: two word runs whose normalised tokens overlap strongly (a set-overlap index over a threshold, with a
  minimum number of shared tokens), close together in time, are treated as a retake; the earlier run is proposed for
  removal (inferred from the evidence's wording).
- **Fillers**: hesitation sounds (long "mm" and "aah" forms) are always proposed; common discourse words (the Arabic
  "uh/that is/what's this" family) only when a pause sits next to them.
- **Arabic normalisation** before comparing: diacritics and tatweel removed, alef forms unified, final ya and ta
  marbuta unified.
- **Sign-off**: nothing is ever cut at or after the recorded sign-off.

## Our version

### What it does
`cut_proposals.propose(paths, profile) -> dict` reads the reviewed words, the audio pause map and the client's filler
profile and writes `01_analysis/cut-proposals.json`. Each proposal:

```json
{"id": "CP-007", "kind": "retake", "start_ms": 41230, "end_ms": 44880,
 "words": ["…"], "keep_ref": "CP-007k", "confidence": 0.86,
 "reason": "اتقالت الجملة مرتين؛ شيلنا الأولى وسبنا الأوضح", "default": "proposed"}
```

Kinds (ours): `pause`, `retake`, `false_start`, `filler`, `repeat_word`. `default` is `proposed` or `keep` (low
confidence). **Nothing is cut** until the editor approves the plan; approved proposals become edit-decisions cuts.

### Rules (ours; numbers are starting points, tuned on our fixtures and stored per client)
1. **Normalise** with the **one shared normaliser in the engine**, `engine/reel/lib/cut_proposals_norm.py`
   (standard library only; the identity scan in `tools/` loads the same file, so the two never drift): NFKC, case-fold, harakat and tatweel removed,
   أإآٱ→ا, ى→ي, ة→ه, ؤ→و, ئ→ي; Latin terms lower-cased; numbers compared as digits (Arabic-Indic = Western).
2. **Pauses**: from `recipes.pause_params` (CLI > recipe > profile > defaults), so Hush, `reel plan` and the
   proposals use one source of truth. Cut points snap to the lowest-energy 10 ms frame within ±50 ms.
3. **Retakes**: a sliding comparison of word runs (2–12 words) inside a 20 s window; similarity = Jaccard on
   normalised tokens **plus** an order bonus (longest common subsequence / run length). Score ≥ 0.65 and ≥ 2 shared
   **content** tokens (fillers do not count) → retake. **We keep the best take, not just the last**: the kept take is the one that is complete (ends at
   a sentence end or a pause ≥ 350 ms), has the higher mean word confidence, and has no filler inside; ties keep the
   later take. The removed take is padded so no breath is cut in half.
4. **False starts**: a word cut off (very short, low confidence, followed within 400 ms by a restart of the same
   stem) is proposed with the restart kept.
5. **Fillers** (Egyptian first, per client, stored in the reel-profile `fillers` list and editable):
   - **always** (pure hesitation sounds, never a word): after normalisation, tokens made only of a hesitation pattern:
     `م{3,}` (ممم…), `ام{3,}` (اممم…), `ا{2,}ه+` (ااه، اااه…), `ه+م{2,}` (همم، هممم…); English `um+`, `u+h+`, `erm+`, `hm+`.
     Note: «اه» (one alef: "yes"), «إيه» ("what") and the 3-letter «امم» (also the word «أمم», "nations") are
     **real words**: never in this list; a token made only of ه («هه»، «هههه»: written laughter) is a **human
     moment**, never a filler and never proposed for a cut;
   - **stretched-only**: the 3-letter «امم» is proposed only when **stretched** (below);
   - **context** (only when **stretched**, with a pause ≥ 250 ms on **both** sides, never at the end of a phrase or a
     question — not followed by ؟ / ? or a sentence end — and never inside a quote): «إيه»، «اه»، يعني، بص، طب،
     فـ; English "like", "you know", "so";
   - **stretched** (measurable): the word's duration is ≥ 1.6 × the clip's median duration for words of the same
     letter count (same speaker) **and** ≥ 350 ms; an ASR confidence below 0.6 raises the proposal's confidence; a
     context word that is not stretched is never proposed;
   - never: the only word of a phrase, a word inside a scene quote, a word the story-editor marked as a key word.
   The default dialect lists are data in a new `config/defaults/cut-proposals.json` (a shared config file, so it is
   added through the executor); per-client overrides live in the reel-profile.
6. **Repeated word**: the same normalised word twice in a row with no meaning change ("في في") → one removed.
7. **Sign-off guard**: the recorded sign-off (from the profile's endings: its phrase, matched on normalised tokens
   in the last 20 s; or the editor's marker) and everything after it are never proposed. If no sign-off is found but
   the profile says there is one, the last 8 s are protected and the editor gets one line.
8. **Protect**: proposals never cut inside a word, inside a quote used by a scene (kinetic/quote text), across a
   speaker change, or into a B-roll anchor; they never produce a kept piece shorter than 300 ms.

### Basis for every threshold
Every number above (the ±50 ms snap, the retake score 0.65 and window, the 1.6× / 350 ms stretch rule, the 250 ms
pause beside a context filler, the 400 ms false-start restart, the 300 ms minimum kept piece, the 8 s / 20 s
sign-off guards) is a **starting value of ours**, stored in `config/defaults/cut-proposals.json` with
`"basis": "starting"` and replaced by `"measured: <fixture set>, <date>"` once the precision/recall run on our
labelled fixtures confirms or changes it.

### Where the editor sees them
- **Premiere markers** on the sequence (colour per kind; name = kind + reason), through the `ppro_build` hook.
- **Panel list** (R-6.5 Review tab): each proposal, seek on click, accept/keep per row, "accept all of this kind".
- `plan.md`: a "Proposed cuts" table with totals (seconds saved per kind).
- The learning loop (T4.3) records which kinds the editor rejects per client and lowers their default to `keep`
  after 3 rejections of the same kind in a row (a suggestion the editor sees, never silent).

### Better than Source-R
Best-take choice instead of "drop the earlier"; confidence and plain reasons per row; dialect lists per client;
markers inside Premiere; one pause source shared with Hush; protections for quotes, speakers and B-roll anchors.

## Acceptance
Transcript fixtures with retakes, false starts, fillers and repeated words in Egyptian Arabic, English and mixed;
the sign-off is never proposed; written laughter («هه»، «هههه») and the real words «اه»، «إيه»، «امم» (unstretched)
are never proposed as fillers; nothing is applied without approval; a **precision/recall table** per kind against
editor labels recorded in evidence (target: retake precision ≥ 0.8, filler precision ≥ 0.9); deterministic output
for the same input.
