# R-4.1 Caption looks: 3 grouping modes × 3 presentations, word emphasis, per-client preference, fit-or-fail

Phase 4, Task 23 (absorbs Task 16, the caption look gallery). Owner: looks track. Shared hooks (`previews.py`,
`ppro_build.py`, `plan.py`, `cli.py`) go through the executor as diffs.

## Source-R behaviour

- **Grouping.** Spoken words are grouped into on-screen captions in one of three modes: a short group of a few words,
  a clause-length group that follows the sentence structure, or a single word at a time.
- **Presentation.** Each group is drawn in one of three presentations: text on a rounded solid shape, bare text with
  a light outline, or text on a thin horizontal strip.
- **Emphasis.** Individual words can be emphasised by an accent colour, an underline or a short scale "pop". The pop
  is refused in looks whose captions are meant to stay static.
- **Fit-or-fail.** Each group is measured with the real font. A group that does not fit the safe width is split at
  word boundaries; a single word that still does not fit **fails the render** with a message naming the word.
- **Visibility.** Captions can be switched off for a video whose source already has burned-in subtitles, while the
  words stay available for cutting (see R-4.2).
- **Preferences.** Grouping, presentation and emphasis are each either "auto" (derived from purpose and tone, e.g. a
  calm explainer gets clause-length groups) or "fixed" to one value. A one-video override never changes the profile.
- **Export.** Captions also export as subtitle files.

## Our version

### 1. Name
Caption looks are named `<grouping>-<presentation>`, our own words:

| Axis | Values (ours) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| grouping | `word` | one word per caption, timed to the word (fast, energetic content) |
| | `phrase` | 2–4 words, never across a pause ≥ 250 ms or a sentence end (today's `captions-short` behaviour, improved) |
| | `line` | one readable line up to the safe width, split at clause boundaries (punctuation, conjunctions, pauses ≥ 350 ms) |
| presentation | `clean` | text with a soft brand-tinted drop plate (a blurred shadow 4 px below, 35 % opacity, no outline stroke) |
| | `boxed` | text on a squared plate (corner radius 6 px at 1080 width) in brand ink-dark with a 6 px **accent foot** (a brand-accent bar along the plate's bottom edge) |
| | `highlight` | the **currently spoken word** gets a brand-accent underlay that sits low (from 35 % of the x-height down to 20 % below the baseline), moving word to word with the word timing |
| emphasis | `colour` | the emphasised word takes the brand highlight colour (contrast-checked) |
| | `weight` | the emphasised word steps up one weight in the same family (Arabic never uses italic) |
| | `tag` | the emphasised word sits on a small rounded tag plate in brand accent (static; the text colour is contrast-stepped against the tag) |

**Static signatures (ours, visible in a still):** the accent foot of `boxed`, the low underlay of `highlight`, the
drop plate of `clean`, the accent tag of `tag`. Source-R's presentations were a rounded solid shape, an outline and a
thin strip; ours differ in a still, not only in motion.

That gives **8 caption looks** (3 × 3 minus the refused `word-highlight`, below), each with an emphasis choice. Task 16's four starter looks map onto them
(`clean` → `phrase-clean`, `box` → `phrase-boxed`, `highlight` → `phrase-highlight`, `bold-large` → `line-clean`
with the look's large size).

**Combinations.** `word-highlight` is refused (a one-word caption has nothing to move between) and resolves to
`word-boxed` with a plan note; every other combination is valid. A motion look with `caption_motion: "none"` (R-6.1)
  shows its captions **with no entrance or exit animation** (they cut in and out on their times) and no moving
  `highlight` (it falls back to `boxed`); the static emphases (`colour`, `weight`, `tag`) stay.

**"auto" with no saved preference.** A client with no `captions` preference resolves to **today's behaviour** (the
existing caption stills: `phrase` grouping from `captions-short`, the client's current `caption_style` keyword
highlight), so the default output stays byte-identical. The recommender (R-4.6) only *suggests* a caption look for
such a client (gallery + one line); the change happens when the editor picks one (`--pick … --yes`) or sets "auto"
explicitly in the profile.

### 2. Motion (captions follow their caption look, not the scene look's motion law)
Spoken-word captions are their own layer: their motion is defined **here**, by the caption look. A motion look
(R-6.1) chooses which caption look its reels use (`caption_look`), but its motion law drives scene graphics only.
- Group entrance: a **focus-in** in place: scale 96 % → 100 % from the group's centre with opacity 0 → 100 %, 6 frames
  (at 30 fps; scaled by fps/30), ease-out; no vertical travel, no blur, no overshoot. Exit: a 4-frame fade.
  (Source-R's signature is a vertical rise; ours never travels.)
- `highlight`: the underlay slides from word to word in the reading direction over 3 frames, eased in-out; it is laid
  out from the shaped glyph runs so it covers the visual word in RTL and LTR.
- Lead-in: a caption may appear up to `lead_in_ms` before its first word (the existing graphics-templates knob), never
  over the previous caption.

**Where the motion is made (per build path):**
- **Premiere (default):** captions are engine-rendered stills (`03_broll/captions/CAP_*_fg.png`, `_bar.png`, as
  today); `ppro_build` animates them with clip keyframes, extending today's `text_pop` / `bar_sweep` keys: Scale and
  Opacity keyframes with Premiere's Ease Out temporal interpolation for the focus-in; the `highlight` underlay is one
  still per word state (or a Crop-keyframed bar), switched on the word's time.
- **After Effects motion builds:** the same timings as AE keyframes with temporal ease (influence 70 %, speed 0) in
  `wnc_motion_lib.jsx`.
- **Native caption track** (`native_caption_track: true` in the client style): our stack only imports an SRT into
  Premiere's caption track (`createCaptionTrack`, CEP; no styling API in UXP or CEP), so the native track is **plain
  SRT with no presentation, no emphasis and no motion**. The editor sees one line saying so and may apply a Premiere
  Track Style by hand; the caption looks apply only to our still-based captions.

### 3. Brand-aware
- Colours come from the client's brand roles (ink, paper, accent, highlight) through the look; nothing is hard-coded.
- Every text/background pair is checked with `legibility.py` (G68) **against the pixels behind the caption** on
  sampled frames, not only the brand swatch. If a pair fails `legibility.needed(size_px)` (today 4.5:1, or 3:1 from `large_px` 64 px) the
  ink/highlight lightness is stepped toward
  contrast; if that fails the look's neutral is used and the editor sees one line ("الكلمة المميزة اتغيّر لونها
  عشان تتقري على الخلفية الفاتحة").
- Fonts: the client's caption font role (`font_roles.py`); the Arabic face is required for Arabic (G72 warning when
  missing).

### 4. Arabic first
- Words are shaped as words; nothing is ever split into letters or animated per glyph.
- Mixed AR/EN groups keep Latin terms as LTR runs inside RTL lines (FSI/PDI isolation in our text builder); a Latin
  term, a number and its unit, or a medical term is never split across groups or lines (the `motion_layout.glue`
  rules).
- **Digits** follow the client's digit-set preference (Western or Arabic-Indic ٠–٩), per client, and the choice
  converts digits **only inside Arabic runs**: Latin runs, model and product names, handles, URLs, e-mail addresses,
  code, file names and version strings always keep Western digits ("GPT-4o", "@brand2024", "v3.0" never change).
- With Arabic-Indic digits, the decimal separator is ٫ (U+066B), the thousands separator ٬ (U+066C) and the percent
  sign ٪ (U+066A): "3.5" → «٣٫٥», "12,000" → «١٢٬٠٠٠», "40%" → «٤٠٪» (stored digits first, then ٪, no space:
  U+0664 U+0660 U+066A, as ICU's ar-EG formatting gives) shaped as one run. With Western digits inside
  Arabic text the ASCII separators stay.
- **Clause punctuation** for grouping and re-grouping includes the Arabic marks ، (comma), ؛ (semicolon) and ؟
  (question mark) as well as . , ; ? ! …
- **Font chain, per script** (the same chain everywhere in R-4.1, R-6.1 and R-6.2): Arabic runs → the client's
  Arabic face for the role → the client's Arabic body face → (a look-level fallback, if the look names one) → IBM
  Plex Sans Arabic (house, OFL). Latin runs → the client's Latin face for the role → the client's Arabic face if it
  has Latin glyphs → (look fallback) → Inter (house, OFL). A mixed line shapes each run with its own font.
- **Missing-glyph check** (inside fit-or-fail): a glyph id 0 (.notdef) from HarfBuzz / CoreText moves that run to
  the next font in the chain; if the house font also lacks it, the plan reports the character as `U+XXXX` with the
  cue time, never a silent box.
- The `highlight` underlay and the `tag` plate follow the visual order of shaped runs, so they land on the right word in RTL.

### 5. Easier
- The gallery `reel caption-looks` renders the looks on two frames of the editor's own clip, so the editor picks by
  sight. `--pick <look>` shows the diff against the client's `reel-profile/style.json` and writes only with `--yes`.
- Per-client preference in `reel-profile/style.json`:
  `"captions": {"grouping": {"mode": "auto"|"fixed", "value": …}, "presentation": {…}, "emphasis": {…}}`.
  "auto" is derived from the content type (R-4.6 recommender) and the measured speech rate (words per second:
  ≥ 3.2 w/s favours `phrase`, ≤ 2.2 w/s favours `line`). A one-reel override lives in that reel's edit-decisions and
  never changes the profile. The learning loop (T4.3) may **suggest** turning auto into fixed after the editor
  picked the same value 3 times in a row; the editor confirms.
- The panel (R-6.6) shows why "auto" chose a value in one line.

### 6. Better quality (fit-or-fail, our way)
Before any build, each caption group is measured with the client's real font (HarfBuzz on Windows, CoreText on Mac)
against the **safe width of the selected safe-zone profile** (R-4.5), not a fixed pixel number:
1. **Re-group**: split the group at the best word boundary (clause punctuation first, then the longest pause, then the
   middle), keeping glued runs whole. Repeat until each part fits or is one glued run.
2. **Shrink**: reduce the size in steps of 2 px down to the floor (the caption look's size × 0.85, never below 44 px
   at a 1080 px frame width, scaled by width). This floor is for spoken-word captions only; scene labels keep the
   look's own type scale (R-6.1).
3. **Hide and report** (last resort, G10: never a blocked build): if a single glued run still does not fit at the
   floor, **that one caption is hidden** (its words stay in the transcript, the cues and the cut proposals) and
   `reel plan` gives a notice naming the cue (start time) and the fix ("too long for one line: choose `word`
   grouping or a narrower look"). It never overflows, wraps into a third line or clips.
Also: captions synced to word timing (sequence clock after the time map), hidden while a scene already shows the
same words (existing `captions_hidden_during`), and the two-line limit of `subtitle-style.json` is respected.

### 7. Distinctiveness check
The gallery sheet (all 8 looks, AR and EN, on a bright and a dark frame) is reviewed by the brand-systems-designer
next to Source-R's own look previews (the captions visible in `WhyNot-Cut-3.0-Addendum/evidence/source-r-frames/`, staged by the plan session; 00-INDEX) and must
be "recognisably ours, clearly better". A "no" is a P1.

## Where it lives
These paths **replace** Task 16's planned `config/defaults/caption-looks.json` and `lib/caption_looks.py` (one
implementation, inside the looks track's locked area; Task 16's interfaces `render(...)` / `pick(...)` keep their

  `engine/reel/lib/looks_captions.py` (grouping, fit-or-fail, gallery render).

- Through the executor: the caption hook in `previews.render_captions` (defaults keep today's output byte-identical),
  `ppro_build.py` caption items, `plan.py` (fit-or-fail errors), `cli.py` (`caption-looks` verb).
- Tests: `tests/test_looks*.py` (AR, EN, mixed RTL; golden still per look; darwin path with renderer fakes).

## Acceptance
AR/EN/mixed RTL tests per grouping; digit tests (conversion only inside Arabic runs; handles, model names and URLs
untouched; ٫ ٬ ٪ with Arabic-Indic digits, including "40%" → «٤٠٪» stored as ٤ ٠ ٪ (digits first, then U+066A,
no space)); grouping breaks at ، ؛ ؟; a font-chain test (mixed line, per-script
fallback, a planted missing glyph reported as U+XXXX); the hide-and-report last resort; a golden-still test per look;
the gallery renders 8 looks × 2 frames in ≤ 20 s on the CPU (a starting budget, measured; over budget = a warning); fit-or-fail tests (re-group, shrink, report naming
the run); the default output stays byte-identical; the gallery sheet is viewed by graphic-checker (Arabic shaping,
safe area) and by the brand-systems-designer (distinctiveness).
