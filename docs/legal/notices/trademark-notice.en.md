> **Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review this text before you send it.** This is a fill-in template: replace every
> double-brace field and delete what does not apply.

{{date}}

To: {{recipient}}
From: {{sender_org}} ({{contact}})

**Subject: Use of the {{our_mark}} name and logo by {{their_product}}**

Dear {{recipient}},

{{sender_org}} owns the name and logo {{our_mark}} (the "mark") and uses them for its software and services. The
source code of WhyNot Cut at {{our_repo}} is free software under the GNU General Public License. That licence covers
the code only. It does not give anyone the right to use our name, logo or branding.

We noticed that {{their_product}}, offered at {{their_location}}, uses the mark or a confusingly similar one. The
evidence we hold is listed under "Attachments" below.

A trademark notice is not a complaint about your right to use the code under the licence. We ask you to:
1. stop using the mark, and any name or logo that can be confused with it, for your product, site and promotion, or
   tell us on what basis you use it;
2. state clearly in your own material that your product is not made, approved or supported by {{sender_org}};
3. answer in writing within {{deadline_days}} days of the date of this letter.

If you only need to say that your work is based on ours, you may name the original project in plain text, in a way
that does not suggest we endorse you.

This letter does not waive any of our rights. Please answer to {{contact}}.

Kind regards,
{{sender_org}}

## Attachments (what to attach before sending)
1. **Commit history**: the log of our repository showing when the mark first appeared in our files (commit ids and
   dates).
2. **Release hashes**: the SHA-256 hash of each of our releases that carries the mark, to show our public use.
3. **Tag names**: the exact tag names of those releases.
4. **Their use**: {{evidence}} (dated screenshots or page saves that show the mark as they use it).
5. **Our registrations or first-use proof**: whatever {{sender_org}} holds, as your lawyer advises.
